# AI 1 & 2 Coursework

Coursework archive from TJHSST's AI 1 and AI 2 classes: search algorithms,
adversarial/turn-based game AI, formal languages & regular expressions, and
supervised learning basics, plus general Python warm-ups and notes on
real-world AI tooling.

Several of the larger, more polished unit projects have been extracted into
their own standalone repositories (see below) — check those out first if
you're looking for the more complete, presentable work. What remains here is
the rest of the coursework, kept together as an archive.

## Flagship projects (extracted to their own repos)

- **[csp-solvers](https://github.com/KuvaKodes/csp-solvers)** — Constraint satisfaction problem solvers (from Unit 2).
- **[railroad-pathfinder-visualizer](https://github.com/KuvaKodes/railroad-pathfinder-visualizer)** — Final project: a pathfinding visualizer over a railroad network (from Unit 4).
- **[genetic-algorithm-cipher-cracker](https://github.com/KuvaKodes/genetic-algorithm-cipher-cracker)** — Genetic algorithm used to crack substitution ciphers (from Unit 6).
- **[kmeans-image-quantization-dithering](https://github.com/KuvaKodes/kmeans-image-quantization-dithering)** — K-means image color quantization and dithering (from Unit 7).
- **[mnist-neural-net-from-scratch](https://github.com/KuvaKodes/mnist-neural-net-from-scratch)** — A neural network built from scratch to classify MNIST digits (from Unit 9).

## What's in this repo

- **Unit 0 Python** — Introductory Python exercises, Project Euler problems, and a heap/set/dict performance exercise over generated integer files.
- **Unit 1 Searches** — Uninformed and informed search algorithms (BFS, bidirectional BFS, iterative-deepening DFS, A*) applied to sliding puzzles, word ladders, and peg solitaire.
- **Unit 3 Turn-Based Games** — Adversarial search / game-playing AI (minimax-style agents) for tic-tac-toe and Othello, including different Othello strategies (greedy, corners-aware, random) and timing comparisons.
- **Unit 5 Regular Expressions** — Regular expressions and finite automata: DFA implementations, regex-based text search, and related demos.
- **Unit 8 Supervised Learning** — Decision tree classifier basics applied to small labeled datasets (mushroom edibility, tennis-playing conditions, restaurant wait times).
- **Unit 10 Real World AI Tools** — Notes and small scripts exploring applied/real-world AI and NLP tooling.
